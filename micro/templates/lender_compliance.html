Lender Compliance Assistance

Starting a lending business in Lesotho can be complex — Fedha-Grow helps lenders become compliant, licensed, and operational with confidence.

The Problem

  • Complex Central Bank requirements
  • High consultant fees (M 50,000 – M 150,000)
  • 6–12 month approval timeline
  • Lack of regulatory understanding
  • Fear of penalties and shutdowns

Our Solution: Lender Compliance Hub

  • Assess eligibility and recommend correct license type
  • Generate required documents automatically
  • Track application status step-by-step
  • Provide ongoing compliance tools (AML, reporting, audits)
  • Allow lenders to operate under Fedha-Grow umbrella during approval

Lender Compliance Hub — How it works

Guided licensing, document generation & CBL tracking for lenders
Overview

The Lender Compliance Hub helps individuals, groups and small firms understand which regulatory path at the Central Bank of Lesotho (CBL) they need, auto-generate required application documents, track the CBL application progress, and provide ongoing compliance tooling (AML, reporting, fit & proper checks).

Problem summary
  • Complex CBL requirements and long approval timelines
  • High consultant costs (M 50k–150k)
  • Small lenders lack compliance capacity
Solution snapshot
  • Eligibility & tier recommendation wizard
  • Auto-document generation (business plans, schedules, fit-and-proper forms)
  • CBL application tracking & status updates
  • Ongoing compliance tooling (reporting, AML checks, credit bureau integration)
Quick Tiers

Tier 1: Individual lenders (facilitated under platform license)
Tier 2: Small MFIs (assets < 2M) — guided registration
Tier 3: Full MFIs (2M+ capital) — full application support
Tier 4: Alternative / peer-to-peer under platform umbrella
