Borrower Groups

Unlock the power of collective trust.

Groups like Societies, Stokvels, and Unions give lenders confidence and borrowers easier access to credit.

Social Proof

Group membership reduces identity fraud and provides credibility to lenders.

Reduced Risk

Lenders feel safer when borrowers are backed by a trusted organization.

Better Access

Borrowers in groups often enjoy faster approvals and better terms.

How it Works

  1. 📌 Register as Group Admin.
  2. 📌 Admin creates a group (society, stokvel, union, etc.).
  3. 📌 The group admin invites members to join.
  4. 📌 Members activate their profiles and link to the group.
  5. 📌 Lenders can subscribe to and finance groups directly.
